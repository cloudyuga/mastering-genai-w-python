# 🤖 Introduction to Generative AI Module

This module gives non-technical managers a fast, plain-language grounding in AI/ML fundamentals and then focuses on what actually matters for this course: what Generative AI is, how it differs from traditional/predictive AI, its business value, and the current market and ecosystem — including where AI hardware/inference costs are headed.

There are no hands-on coding labs in this module — delivery is slides plus a live demo, matching the course's "no prerequisites, non-technical audience" design.

---

## 📊 Slides

`presentations/For Managers - Introduction to AI - Presentation.pdf` (also maintained as an editable Canva design). Covers, in order: AI/ML/DL primer → What is Generative AI → GenAI vs traditional AI → Business Value of GenAI → AI Infrastructure & Hardware Advances → Current Market & Ecosystem. A handful of more technical slides (Narrow vs General AI, perceptron/activation-function math, MNIST network internals) are kept as an appendix at the end for reference, not for live delivery.

## 🎥 Live Demo

Traditional/narrow AI vs. Generative AI, side by side: the same request answered first by a simple rule-based/classifier response, then by a GenAI chat model. Reuses the Gradio chatbot work from Module 3/4 rather than a separate build.

## 🗄️ Archived Notebooks

`archive-dev-notebooks/` holds three notebooks from the original developer-track version of this module (`House_Price_Prediction_LR.ipynb`, `House_Price_Prediction_Multiple_Variable_LR.ipynb`, `Handwritten_Digit_Classification_MNIST.ipynb`, plus their `train.csv` dataset). They're kept for optional reference — e.g. if a participant wants to see the regression/neural-net math underneath the "traditional AI" slides — but are not part of the standard delivery.
