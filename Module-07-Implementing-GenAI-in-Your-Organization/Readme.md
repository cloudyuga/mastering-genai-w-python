# 🚀 Implementing GenAI in Your Organization

The capstone module: how to actually take GenAI from a demo to a deployed
capability — internal AI strategy, buy vs. build vs. partner, team
structure and roles, integrating AI into existing ERP/CRM/HRMS systems, and
setting realistic ROI expectations.

---

## 📊 Slides

Maintained as a Canva design ("Module 7: Implementing GenAI in Your
Organization"). Covers why most AI initiatives fail, building an AI
strategy, the buy/build/partner decision framework, building an AI team,
integrating agents into ERP/CRM/HRMS (with a 2026 Salesforce–Google Cloud
case study), realistic ROI expectations, a practical getting-started
roadmap, and closes with a recap of the full 7-module course.

## 🧪 Labs

This module is strategy-focused and has no dedicated coding labs of its
own — it draws on live demos already built in earlier modules (particularly
the agent and RAG demos from Modules 3 and 5) to illustrate what a deployed,
integrated GenAI capability looks like in practice.
